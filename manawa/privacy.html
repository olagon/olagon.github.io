Manawa privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Manawa is a set of clock widgets in ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi, made by Kealoha Labs. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What Manawa collects

Nothing. Manawa has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What stays on your phone

The choices you make for each widget (theme, background, colors, and moon size) and for the bedside clock are saved on your phone so your widgets look the same after a restart. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling Manawa deletes them.

Location

Manawa does not use your location. Tonight's moon and the Hawaiian night names are worked out on your phone for Honolulu, from the date and time.

Children

Manawa collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
