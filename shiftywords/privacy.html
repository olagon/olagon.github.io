ShiftyWords privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

ShiftyWords is a daily word puzzle for Android, made by Kealoha Labs. It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What ShiftyWords collects

Nothing. ShiftyWords has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What stays on your phone

Today's game in progress, your streaks and stats for each language, your chosen language, and your reminder setting are saved on your phone so they survive a restart. They never leave your phone, and uninstalling ShiftyWords deletes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, Android asks your permission to show notifications. Your phone schedules and shows the reminder. No server is involved. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, ShiftyWords makes a picture of your colored grid and hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice. ShiftyWords doesn't send it anywhere itself.

Children

ShiftyWords collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
