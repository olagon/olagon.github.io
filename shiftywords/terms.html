ShiftyWords terms of use

Effective September 29, 2026

Be kind. That's the heart of how this puzzle should be used. Be kind to the languages you play in and the people who carry them. Everything else on this page is the detail.

Using ShiftyWords

ShiftyWords for Android is free. You don't need to sign up, sign in, or pay anything. Your streaks and progress live on your phone. The original web game at shiftywords.com is open source under the MIT License.

What we ask of you

Don't try to break the app or misuse it. Don't copy the word lists to build a competing product without credit. Treat the languages with respect. Each one belongs to a living community, so play it as a guest.

Accuracy of the words

Every word is checked against a dictionary for its language, with ʻokina, kahakō, and accents where they belong. Languages are alive and dictionaries disagree, so you may see a word you'd spell or define differently. If you spot an error, please tell us.

No warranty

ShiftyWords is provided "as is." We do our best, but we can't promise it will always work, that your streak will never reset, or that every word is perfect. Play it for fun.

Changes

If these terms ever change, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
